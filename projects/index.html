GEO: Global Education Observatory

A geocoded school database covering 23+ low- and middle-income countries, integrating Education Management Information Systems (EMIS) data with satellite-derived covariates. GEO supports research on educational equity, resource allocation, and learning outcomes across Sub-Saharan Africa, Southeast Asia, and beyond. Developed in collaboration with regional partners through the GEDEx Spencer Foundation RPP.

Push-and-Pull Factors in Cross-Border Migration with Deep Learning

Modeling international migration flows using deep learning to identify and quantify push and pull factors across origin-destination country pairs. The project leverages large-scale socioeconomic, environmental, and conflict datasets to understand the drivers of cross-border movement — with implications for humanitarian response and migration policy.
